    About

    PIPELINE & PRACTICE

    I cut picture for brands, broadcasters and independent directors — work that has to hold attention without shouting for it.

    Ten years across commercials, documentary and long-form interview. My approach is structural: find the hook, decide the rhythm before touching a single transition, then protect it through grade and mix. I work in Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve daily, finish in After Effects, and treat sound as part of the edit rather than a layer added at the end.

    Available for remote and on-site post-production. Typical turnaround for a 60-second commercial: 3 working days to first cut.
